Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

Welcome to CrustyRustacean. This privacy policy explains how this personal blog handles your information. This is a personal, non-commercial website, and I am committed to protecting your privacy.

Information Collection

This website collects minimal information necessary to provide basic functionality:

  • Account Information: If you choose to register, we store your username, email address, and an encrypted password. This information is stored solely for your convenience to enable features like posting articles and managing favorites.
  • Profile Information: You may optionally provide a bio and profile image. This information is entirely optional and under your control.
  • Content: Any articles, comments, or other content you create on the site.

How Information Is Used

The information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Provide account functionality and allow you to log in
  • Display your profile and content you create
  • Enable site features like favorites and following other users

I do not:

  • Sell, rent, or share your information with third parties
  • Use your information for marketing purposes
  • Track your browsing behavior across other websites
  • Collect payment information (this site does not process any payments)

Data Security

Your password is securely hashed using industry-standard encryption before being stored. I take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, but please be aware that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses browser storage (cookies and local storage) to maintain your login session. This is strictly for functionality purposes and to keep you logged in between visits. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Bootstrap CSS: For styling and layout (loaded from CDN)
  • Font Awesome: For icons (loaded from CDN)

These services may have their own privacy policies and may collect information about your visit. I recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies if you have concerns.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information stored about you
  • Update or correct your information through your account settings
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time
  • Opt out of receiving any communications from the site

To exercise these rights or if you have questions, please contact me through the social links provided on the site.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
